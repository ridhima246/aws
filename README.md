AttorneyCare

AttorneyCare is a legal workflow and accountability platform that transforms contracts into structured, clause-level negotiation systems with complete traceability and AI-assisted risk detection.

 In law, how a decision was made is often more important than the decision itself.

Problem

Legal negotiations today happen across:
- Word documents  
- emails  
- calls  
- messaging apps  

When disputes arise, organizations struggle to prove:
- who approved what  
- what changed  
- when it changed  
- who was aware of the risks  

 Solution

AttorneyCare converts documents into **trackable legal workflows**.

Each clause becomes:
- independently reviewable  
- discussable  
- versioned  
- auditable  

AI assists by flagging issues and simplifying language, while humans retain full authority.

Core Capabilities (MVP)

✔ Document upload  
✔ Automatic clause structuring  
✔ Clause status (Agreed / Negotiation / Rejected)  
✔ Comments & suggestions  
✔ Version history  
✔ AI explanations  
✔ Risk indicators  
✔ Immutable activity trail  

High-Level Architecture

Frontend → API → Services → Storage → Audit

Key design focus:
- traceability  
- accountability  
- explainability  

Why It Matters

Years later, courts and regulators ask:

**How was this agreement reached?**

AttorneyCare provides the answer.
